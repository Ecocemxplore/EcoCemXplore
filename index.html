About

EcoCemXplore-Project: Exploring Eco-efficient blended cements innovations in performance and sustainability

Building a low-carbon future starts with transforming the materials we rely on most. EcoCemXplore aims to develop innovative, sustainable cements that could cut emissions by up to 40% compared to traditional Portland cement. To achieve this, the project brings together materials science, civil engineering, and environmental science to design ultra-low-clinker hybrid alkaline cements that use treated low-purity clays. The project investigates ways to enhance clay reactivity and optimise fresh-state properties, as well as understanding phase evolution over time, through advanced characterisation techniques and thermodynamic modelling. Ultimately, the project will identify key strategies for promoting the use of low-cost clays in developing eco-efficient, low-carbon cements.

About me

Materials Science and Engineering researcher specialising in sustainable cementitious materials, with a focus on eco-efficient alternatives to Portland cement, alkali-activated and hybrid binders, and the durability and corrosion behaviour of reinforced systems. Research combines advanced electrochemical techniques and micro- and nanoscale characterisation to understand degradation mechanisms and improve long-term performance of construction materials. Currently a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Bath, leading the EcoCemXplore project, which investigates low-clinker hybrid cements, reaction mechanisms, phase evolution, and durability using advanced experimental approaches, including synchrotron-based techniques. Holds a PhD (Cum Laude) from the University Carlos III of Madrid, with extensive experience in eco-efficient construction materials and corrosion science. Previous research positions include the Institute of Ceramics and Glass (CSIC), TU Delft, the University Carlos III of Madrid, ICAI (Universidad Pontificia Comillas), and the Escuela Superior Politécnica de Chimborazo (ESPOCH), alongside teaching experience in Spain and Ecuador. Has contributed to multiple international research projects and collaborations across Europe and beyond, resulting in peer-reviewed publications in leading journals in construction materials and corrosion science.

Research Activities

EcoCemXplore

We are currently developing innovative eco-efficient blended cement systems as part of the EcoCemXplore project. Our research focuses on designing low-carbon, ultra-low-clinker hybrid alkaline cements using treated low-purity clays. We investigate material reactivity, fresh-state performance, and long-term phase evolution through experimental testing, advanced characterisation techniques, and thermodynamic modelling

Workability of Eco-efficient Cements

Investigating the fresh-state properties of eco-efficient cements, including rheology, setting time, and early-age strength development, to optimize mix designs for practical applications.

RILEM Convention

Conference presentation on the durability of eco-efficient cements.

Microstructural Analysis

Using advanced microscopy techniques to analyze the microstructure of eco-efficient cements and understand their performance.

Training courses

Participation in specialised training courses aimed at strengthening skills in cement chemistry, sustainable construction materials, advanced characterisation techniques, and thermodynamic modelling. These courses support the development of expertise relevant to eco-efficient cement research.
